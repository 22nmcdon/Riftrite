Riftrite - playtest build
========================================

WINDOWS
  1. Unzip, then double-click Riftrite.exe.
  2. The build isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you the
     first time: click "More info", then "Run anyway".

MACOS
  1. Unzip, then move Riftrite.app somewhere (Applications is fine).
  2. The build isn't signed by a paid Apple developer account, so the first
     time macOS will block it. Right-click (or Control-click) Riftrite.app and
     choose Open, then Open again. On newer macOS, if there's no Open button:
     try to open it once, then go to System Settings > Privacy & Security
     and click "Open Anyway" near the bottom.

PLAYING
  The game is being rebuilt around heroes and an open arena. This build is
  Practice: the Act 1 fights, one at a time, with placeholder art.

  1. Title > Practice, then pick a fight ("Place your heroes").
  2. Drag Brannoc, Maren, and Vell onto your side's hexes (the three columns
     on the left; the enemies are on the right). Hover an enemy to read what
     it does; click a hero to read them.
  3. Tactics: click a hero while placing and pick a tactic for them in
     their card, or None. Each hero takes at most one. Each is a trade: a
     way of behaving that costs something, and a small payoff while it
     applies (new in this build):
       Casters first     goes for the enemy casters (Cinder Moths, Gloam
                         Witches) before anything else, and hits them 20%
                         harder
       Hold your ground  stays put, fighting what's in reach, until an enemy
                         comes within 2 hexes; attacks 20% faster while it
                         holds
       Wait to heal      (Vell) saves Mend until an ally is below 60% HP,
                         and the saved Mend heals 30% more
     The tactic shows under the hero's name, and over them in the fight when
     it does something ("Holds its ground", "Moves out", "Mend waits"). The
     log notes each hit or heal a payoff made bigger ("+20% from Casters
     first").
  4. Fight. It plays by itself:
       Space   pause            1 2 3   speed 0.5x, 1x, 2x
       S       skip to the end  L       open or close the combat log
       T       every target line (for testing)
     The damage chart is on the right. Click a unit to see only its lines in
     the log. Pause and click a hero for their details.
  5. At the end: Rematch (same placement, new crits), Watch again, or
     Place again to try another formation. Back goes to the list.

  Nothing is saved: your formation, tactics, and speed last until you close
  the game.

WHAT WE WANT TO KNOW (playtest gate 1, and tactics)
  Is a single fight fun to set up and watch? Can you tell who is attacking
  whom, and why you won or lost? Which fights felt unfair or confusing?
  Tactics: do they change how a fight plays, and can you see why? Is any of
  them always right, or never worth taking? Do the payoffs make each one
  worth trying in the right fight, and does Wait to heal have a use now?
