Riftrite - playtest build
========================================

WINDOWS
  1. Unzip, then double-click Riftrite.exe.
  2. The build isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you the
     first time: click "More info", then "Run anyway".

MACOS
  1. Unzip, then move Riftrite.app somewhere (Applications is fine).
  2. The build isn't signed by a paid Apple developer account, so the first
     time macOS will block it. Right-click (or Control-click) Riftrite.app and
     choose Open, then Open again. On newer macOS, if there's no Open button:
     try to open it once, then go to System Settings > Privacy & Security
     and click "Open Anyway" near the bottom.

PLAYING
  The game is being rebuilt around heroes and an open arena. This build is
  Practice: the Act 1 fights, one at a time, with placeholder art.

  1. Title > Practice, then pick a fight ("Place your heroes").
  2. Drag Brannoc, Maren, and Vell onto your side's hexes (the bottom three
     rows). Hover an enemy to read what it does; click a hero to read them.
  3. Fight. It plays by itself:
       Space   pause            1 2 3   speed 0.5x, 1x, 2x
       S       skip to the end  L       show or hide the log and chart
       T       every target line (for testing)
     Click a unit to see only its lines in the log. Pause and click a hero
     for their details.
  4. At the end: Rematch (same placement, new crits), Watch again, or
     Place again to try another formation. Back goes to the list.

  Nothing is saved: your formation and speed last until you close the game.

WHAT WE WANT TO KNOW (playtest gate 1)
  Is a single fight fun to set up and watch? Can you tell who is attacking
  whom, and why you won or lost? Which fights felt unfair or confusing?
