# data/heroes/

One folder per hero, each with the same files (`hero.json`, `paths.json`, `upgrades.json`, and `statuses.json` when it has statuses of its own); each folder's README lists its paths, apexes, cards, and bonds. `roster.json` is the order heroes are listed and act in a fight.

Kestra's companions (`grit`, `brindle`, `viper`, `nestling`) are kits inside her `hero.json` and `paths.json`, not heroes of their own; their figures are in `art/figures/heroes/`.
