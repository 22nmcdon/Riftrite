# Events

Status: **agreed in discussion (2026-09-30); built in phase 5c step 8c** (`rebuild-phase5c-combos.md`, section 16.14; the open questions below answered by Decision 43). The scenes behind the Event node (`days-and-nodes.md`). Relics have no downsides (`relics/README.md`), so the game's trade-offs live here. **Numbers are placeholders.**

## Rules

- **Every event has a free "walk away" choice.**
- **Every choice gives something real.** Costs are temporary (a fight, the act) or chosen by you (which hero takes the wound); nothing punishes you for a choice you didn't make.
- The tone is the rift's: grim, quiet, strange.

## Bloodied Oath

An Event node can be a **Bloodied Oath**: it offers **2 oaths, each already assigned to a random hero.** Take one, or pass. The burden lasts **2 fights.**

| Oath | Burden (2 fights) | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Oath of Blood** | −25% max HP | Deed progress ×2 in those fights |
| **Oath of Silence** | Their signature never fires | Deed progress ×2, and +20% ATK and MGK |
| **Oath of the Vanguard** | Must be placed in the front row, and can't hold a tactic | Deed progress ×2, and +20 DEF |
| **Oath of Blood Price** | Every fall in those fights is 2 wounds | Deed progress ×2 |

## Scenes

| Event | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **The Kneeling Knight** | A dead hero of another band, still kneeling. **Take his blade:** a random charm at rank II; a hero of your choice takes a wound. **Bury him:** clears every wound on the team |
| **The Rift Merchant** | A stall with no keeper. **Take a relic:** a random epic; the next shop's prices are +50%. **Leave coin:** pay 10 shards for a random rare |
| **Whispering Stones** | **Listen:** a hero of your choice gains a large chunk of deed progress; their signature is Silenced in the next fight. **Smash them:** +8 shards |
| **A Bleeding Tear** | **Reach in:** a random relic of a random tier (common to legendary). **Seal it:** tomorrow's fight is skipped and counts as a win, at half pay |
| **The Old Well** | **Drink:** +1 rank to a random item you own; a random hero loses 10% max HP for the rest of the act. **Throw in a coin (5 shards):** one item's rank-up progress fills to its next rank |
| **Carrion Birds** | **Drive them off:** fight a small swarm now for shards; losing doesn't count. **Let them feed:** nothing |
| **The Mirror Pool** | **Look in:** swap one hero's vow to another path, keeping half its deed progress. **Look away:** nothing |
| **Ashes of a Band** | **Search the ashes:** 2 random items (charms, tactics, or sigils). **Say their names:** clears every wound on the team, and +5% max HP next fight |

## Answered (Decision 43, 2026-10-01)

- An Event node is a Bloodied Oath **1 time in 4**.
- Whispering Stones gives **a third of the vowed path's deed threshold**, never past it.
- The Mirror Pool **can't take a transformed hero.**
- A Bleeding Tear's seal **isn't offered when tomorrow is an elite or the boss.**
