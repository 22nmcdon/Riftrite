# Boss relics (11)

**After each boss: choose 1 of 3 · free.** Each **rewrites a rule of the game**, far stronger than anything sold in a shop. A boss relic multiplies an engine you've built; it must never win a run on its own. Rules for all relics: `README.md`. Numbers are placeholders.

| Relic | Effect | The rule it rewrites |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Chain of Echoes** | Every chain in the game goes 4 steps deeper, and each step is +15% stronger than the step before (see `README.md`, rule 6) | How far chains go, and whether they fade |
| **Crown of the Hollow King** | Every keyword heroes apply is applied twice: double stacks, or double duration | One application per effect |
| **The Second Sun** | Signatures fire twice | One cast per signature |
| **Rift-Bound Heart** | Every growing upgrade and relic grows twice as fast | How fast scaling grows |
| **Everflame** | Keywords heroes apply never expire during a fight, and enemies can't remove them | Statuses wear off |
| **The Unbending** | Heroes are immune to every harmful status enemies apply, including Stun, Silence, and damage over time. Each one blocked gives that hero +1% DEF and +1% max HP for the rest of the fight | Enemies can disable your heroes |
| **Riftwalker's Soles** | Heroes take no damage from crumbled ground, and enemies standing on it take 5% of their max HP per second | The collapse hurts everyone |
| **The Hollow Throne** | Take 2 cards from every after-fight pick instead of 1 | One upgrade per fight |
| **The Hollow Covenant** | Each hero uses the team's highest value of every basic stat (HP, ATK, MGK, DEF, CRIT, attack speed) | Each hero has their own stats |
| **The Long Watch** | Fights never time out. From 60s on, heroes gain +10% to all basic stats every 10s | Fights end in a tie at 180s |
| **Snaring Shot** | Every 3rd basic attack by a ranged hero Roots its target for 1s. It can't stack or extend an existing Root | Only some heroes control the field |

## Notes

- **Chain of Echoes and the chains it touches:** trigger chains, Crown of Stars, Shared Pain, The Hungering Rift, Overcharge. With Shared Pain, each echo is 10% weaker but 15% stronger from Chain of Echoes, so the echoes grow slightly instead of fading.
- **Riftwalker's Soles** relies on the crumbled-ground rule decided with the relics (`README.md`, Decisions): crumbled ground is walkable, and standing on it deals flat damage every second (`../rebuild-arena.md`).
- **Cut ideas**, kept for later: a double transformation (a hero fills two paths and gets both), and a second placement in the middle of a fight.
