# Gambits (6)

**12 shards · one per hero.** A gambit bends a placement or fight-start rule. It takes one of the hero's loadout slots, and a hero can hold only one. Rank II after using it in 3 fights, rank III after 6 more; buying a copy skips a rank. Rules: `README.md`. Numbers are placeholders.

| Gambit | Rank I | Rank II | Rank III |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Infiltrate** | Can be placed on any hex of the neutral middle row | Also the front row of the enemy's zone | Starts the fight with a Shield of 15% of its max HP |
| **Ambush** | Starts the fight hidden for 3s; its first attack is a crit | Hidden for 4s; that crit deals +50% damage | That first attack also Marks its target for 4s |
| **Rear Guard** | Can be placed one row behind your zone, past the board's normal edge | +20% attack speed for the first 5s | Stays hidden until an enemy comes within 2 hexes |
| **Late Arrival** | Starts off the board and enters at 5s on an edge hex you chose, hidden for 2s | Enters at 4s, with +20% ATK and MGK for 5s | Its first attack Stuns for 1s |
| **Stand Together** | Can share a hex with another hero at placement; they start side by side | Both get +10 DEF for the first 5s | Both get +10% ATK and MGK while within 1 hex of each other |
| **Switch Places** | At 10s, swaps places with the ally farthest from it | Choose the moment: 5s, 10s, or 15s | Both heroes get a Shield of 10% of their max HP on the swap |

## Notes

- **Infiltrate** is meant for a hero pushed forward (usually a tank), so its rank III is a Shield rather than Stealth.
- **Late Arrival** dodges the opening burst entirely; **Stand Together** pays off bunching up, which enemy areas punish.
- Left out on purpose: anything that forces enemies to target a hero (Taunt, Brannoc's) or adds a leap (Ironbrand's).
