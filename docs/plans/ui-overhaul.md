# UI Overhaul: layout, screen art, and animated fights

> Status: **approved (September 2026)**, being built in the order in section 7. Decisions are under **Decided**, at the end.
> Builds on `docs/ui-asset-design.md` (art direction, palette, shape language) and `docs/plans/first-ui.md` (what exists now). Where this plan changes a decision in `ui-asset-design.md`, it says so under **Changes to earlier decisions**, and that doc gets updated once this plan is approved.

---

## 1. Why: what's hard to follow right now

From screenshots of every screen (`tools/ui_screenshots.gd`):

| Problem | Where | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Everything is small text on dark panels, with no visual hierarchy | All screens | Hard to tell what matters or what to click next |
| The Inspector takes a fixed ~25% of the width and is mostly empty | Caravan, stops, fight preview | Wasted space, and your eyes have to jump right to read anything |
| Hero rows, stash, essences, relics, and synergies are always shown as stacked rows | Caravan, stops | The screen's actual job (buying, picking a stop) gets squeezed into the top third |
| The shop is a small cluster in the top-left, with wares and hero recruits mixed together | Caravan | Doesn't look or feel like a shop; lots of empty screen |
| Fights are cards with HP bars and a scrolling text log | Fight | You read the log to learn what happened; nothing looks like a fight |
| Items are drawn icons at 26 px (now art at 38 px, for 16 items) | Everywhere | Items are hard to tell apart |
| Portraits are a letter in a circle | Everywhere | Heroes and enemies have no identity |
| Title and hero pick are plain text on a blank background | Title, run start | No mood and no first impression |
| Status/toast text sits at the bottom in the middle, far from what caused it | All screens | Easy to miss |

## 2. Goals

1. **One job per screen.** Each screen's main action fills the middle of the screen. The guild is always at the bottom, in a compact form.
2. **Details on demand.** Hover an item to get a popup. Click a hero to get their sheet. No always-on Inspector.
3. **Fights you can watch.** Characters attack, get hit, and fall on screen. The log becomes optional.
4. **Real screen art in the item-icon style.** Smooth SVGs (bold shapes, ink outline, top-left light), generated by scripts in `tools/art/`, with the palette from `ui-asset-design.md`.

## 3. New screen layout

The standard frame, for every screen during a run (1920×1080):

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| TOP BAR: Act · Day 3 of 6 · [Caravan > Stop > Fight] · Gold · Keys · Losses |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                                                          |
|                  MAIN STAGE (the screen's job)                           |
|         Caravan wares / stop cards / event / fight arena                 |
|                                                                          |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| GUILD BAR:  [hero][hero][hero][hero][+]   | stash (6) | essences | relics |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 3.1 Guild bar (bottom, on every run screen)

- Each hero is a **portrait token**: their face, rank letter, a small HP/role cue, and a fielded/backup marker. Backup heroes sit dimmed after a divider.
- **Click a hero** to open the **Hero sheet** (3.2).
- **Drop an item on a hero token** to equip it into their first free slot. The token glows green or red while you drag, the same rule as today's drop targets.
- Stash, essence pouch, and relics become compact trays on the right of the bar. Each one opens a larger popover on click.
- Synergies found: a small book icon that opens a list.

### 3.2 Hero sheet (popup panel)

Opens above the guild bar, over the main stage, and closes with Esc or a click outside.

- Large portrait or figure, name, class, rank, specialization.
- Stats (HP, ATK, MGK, DEF, CRIT, ATSP) with icons, and basic attack and Backup text.
- **Their item row, full size.** This is where items are rearranged and infused (drag and drop as today).
- Fielded/backup toggle, front/back row, move left/right.
- Arrows to switch to the next hero without closing.

### 3.3 Item popups (hover)

- Hovering any item (owned, for sale, on an enemy, in a fight) shows a **tooltip card** next to it, not in a side panel.
- Card: art, name, rarity frame, tier, size, tags, cooldown, what it does (the numbers with their breakdown), infusion and level, spill direction, and sell price.
- Same popup for relics, essences, statuses, and enemies. Hold Shift (or pin) to keep it open.
- This replaces the Inspector. Its "what can I do with this" hints move into the popup's footer.

### 3.4 The Caravan (shop), full width

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| TOP BAR                                                                  |
+----------------------------------------------+---------------------------+
|  WARES (on a cloth stall, large item cards)  |  FOR HIRE (recruit cards, |
|  [item][item][item]                          |  full-body figures)       |
|  [item][item]                                |  [hero]   [hero]          |
|                                              |                           |
|   (lever) Reroll 1g        (coin dish) Sell  |      [ Leave Caravan > ]  |
+----------------------------------------------+---------------------------+
| GUILD BAR                                                                |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

- Wares are **large cards** (art at ~96 px) with a price tag, grouped on a stall background. A ware that combines with one you hold gets the brass glow.
- Recruits are standing figures with rank and class, and hovering shows stats.
- Reroll is a lever, and selling is a coin dish you drop items onto (both from `ui-asset-design.md` 6.5).
- Buy by clicking, or by dragging a ware onto a hero token.

### 3.5 Other screens

- **Title:** painted rift/guildhall backdrop, logo placeholder, and large buttons.
- **Run start:** three heroes as standing figures on pedestals, then three starting-package cards.
- **Stop choice:** 3 big illustrated stop cards (Forge, Loot, Vault, Retrain, Event, Upgrade) with icons.
- **Stop / Event:** illustrated parchment card in the middle with the choice buttons.
- **Fight preview:** the arena with enemies placed, their items on hover, and a big "Fight!" button.
- **Rewards / run end:** ledger card (stitched cloth): result, drops as cards, damage meter as bars.

## 4. Animated fights

The combat sim doesn't change (CLAUDE.md rules 1 and 2). The fight screen already replays the fight by stepping the sim, and the log entries say who did what to whom. The animations are driven by those entries.

### Level 1: animated rows (proposed first)

- The arena is a **stage**: enemies at the top and the guild at the bottom, in their front and back rows (the current formation), on a rift-floor background.
- Each unit is a **standing figure** with an HP bar, shield, and status pips under their feet, and small item icons with cooldown sweeps beside them.
- Animations are **tweens on the figure's parts** (no frame-by-frame sheets): idle bob, melee **lunge** toward the target and back, ranged **projectile** (arrow, dart, bolt) flying to the target, magic **cast** glow and orb, **hit** flash, knockback shake, damage number, **heal** sparkle, **shield** ripple, status tick puffs (burn embers, poison bubbles), **death** (fall, fade to grey), and **Rift Collapse** (screen crack overlay, pulse per tick).
- Which animation plays comes from the item's tags (melee, ranged, magic, healing, defense), so new items animate with no extra work.
- The log becomes a collapsible drawer. Speed controls stay.

### Level 2: Guildrun-style arena (later)

- Units **move** on a hex grid, walk into range, and get pushed. This needs the **hex arena in the sim** (positions, movement, range), which `design.md` lists as the target but says to build only once asked. It's a sim change, with its own plan and determinism tests.
- The Level 1 figures, animations, and effects carry straight over.

## 6. Code changes (by area)

- `src/ui/ui_screen.gd` and `main.gd`: new standard frame (top bar, main stage, guild bar), shared by every run screen.
- New widgets: `guild_bar.gd`, `hero_token.gd`, `hero_sheet.gd`, `item_popup.gd` (replaces `inspector.gd` for hovers), `tray_popover.gd`.
- `src/ui/screens/*`: each screen fills the main stage only; the Caravan is rebuilt as in 3.4.
- Fight: `fight_screen.gd` gets an arena stage; new `unit_figure.gd` (figure plus HP/status/items) and `fight_fx.gd` (plays animations from log entries). `fight_player.gd` stays as is.
- `ui_style.gd`: theme with the new panels, buttons, and fonts.
- Tests: update `tests/ui/*` for the new widgets (hero sheet opens on click, drop on a hero token equips, popup shows an item's text, fight FX picks the animation from tags). Sim and run tests are unchanged.

## 7. Proposed order

1. **Frame and guild bar, hero sheet, item popups** (removes the Inspector and frees the screen). **Done:** `GuildBar`, `HeroToken`, `HeroSheet`, `HoverCard`; the Inspector is now the item panel that pops up while an item is selected. The sheet docks above the bar rather than floating over the stage.
2. **Caravan rebuilt full width** plus chrome assets (panels, buttons, fonts, icons). **Done:** fonts (Young Serif headings, Work Sans text; OFL, in `art/fonts/`), chrome (`art/ui/chrome/`: oak, parchment, slate, stall, and bar panels; button plaques) and 20 UI icons (`art/ui/icons/`: stats, gold, keys, losses, day, stops, fight kinds), all from `tools/art/ui_art.py`. The Caravan is a stall of large ware cards beside a For hire column; stops are big icon cards; the day bar and hero sheet use the icons.
3. **Character figures** (8 heroes and 11 enemies) and portraits. **Done:** `tools/art/characters.py` writes each character's body, held layer (it swings around the hand; beasts have none), and round portrait, plus `rig.json`. `CharacterArt` loads them; `Figure` draws a figure (flip, lunge offset, swing, squash, hit flash, fallen); portraits show in the guild bar, recruits, enemy previews, and fight cards; the hero sheet shows the full figure.
4. **Fight Level 1** (animated rows).
5. **Other screens:** title, run start, stop choice, events, rewards.
6. Remaining item icons and relic icons.
7. (Later, own plan) Fight Level 2: hex arena in the sim.

Each step lands as its own PR, with screenshots, and all tests passing.

## 8. Changes to earlier decisions (`ui-asset-design.md`)

- **6.4, "no hero tabs, every hero's row always visible":** replaced by the guild bar and hero sheet. Items move between heroes by dragging onto a hero token, or inside the hero sheet.
- **6.3 / 6.4, the pinned Inspector:** replaced by hover popups.
- **4, Typography (pixel fonts):** replaced by smooth fonts, to match the smooth-art decision.
- **11, "full battle sprites are outside the UI scope":** now in scope (Level 1 figures).

## 9. Decided

1. **Fights:** Level 1 (animated rows) in this pass. The Level 2 hex arena comes later, with its own plan.
2. **Items are managed in the hero sheet popup**, and an item can also be dropped onto a hero's token to equip it.
3. **Characters are full-body figures** animated by parts, with portrait crops for the guild bar.
4. **Order:** the frame, guild bar, hero sheet, and item popups first (section 7).
5. **Fonts:** Claude picks free (OFL) fonts; they can be swapped later.
6. **Hero looks:** designed from each hero's name, class, and items; they can be changed later.
