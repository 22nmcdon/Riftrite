# The Magpie

Status: **agreed in discussion (2026-09-30); his stall built in phase 5c step 6e** (`rebuild-phase5c-combos.md`, section 14.16: two rank II charms, a relic at 25% off, buying relics, one swap a visit). He's a node since step 8a (section 16.4): from day 3, at most twice an act. The Magpie is a node (`days-and-nodes.md`): a rare stall run by a scavenger who takes what fell bands left behind in the rift. Replaces part 6, section 8's Magpie (`rebuild-between-fights.md`). **Grafts are cut**; see the bottom of this file. **Numbers are placeholders.**

## When he shows up

- A node option from **day 3** on, **at most twice per act.**
- **One look:** no rerolls. What he has is what he has.

## His stall

| Stall | What it does | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **2 charms at rank II** | Two random charms (`loadout/charms.md`), already at rank II. Buying one you own skips it to rank III, as usual | 12 shards each |
| **1 stolen relic** | Always **epic or legendary**, at **25% off** | Epic 15, legendary 22 |
| **Buys relics** | Sell him a relic you own for half its tier's price. **The only place in the game to sell a relic** | He pays: common 2, rare 6, epic 10, legendary 15, boss 15 |
| **The swap** | Once per visit, trade a relic you own for a random relic of the **same tier**, free. A boss relic swaps for another boss relic | Free |

- **He doesn't buy items.** Charms, tactics, sigils, and gambits are sold at the shop (below).
- The relic buying and the swap are why you'd pick him over a Camp or a Rift Tear: they let you undo a relic that stopped fitting the build.

## Selling items at the shop

(The shop is the Pedlar.) **Any shop buys items** (charms, tactics, sigils, gambits) for **half their price**, rounded down. The item's rank doesn't change what it sells for. The shop never buys relics.

## Grafts are cut

Grafts (part 6, section 8; part 7b, section 6) were meant to be the one slotted thing that gives a hero a new ability, but charms already do that (Bramble Knot roots, Ember-Tipped burns), take the same slot, and rank up the same way. The best grafts became charms: **Smoke Vial, Sidestep, Iron Skin, Spite Brand, Bloodletter,** and **Scavenger** (`loadout/charms.md`). Grasping Earth was cut. Rally, Beacon, Cleansing Breath, and Mirror Ward are set aside for later.

The graft rule moves to every loadout item: **nothing grants a path's key mechanic or undoes a path's cost** (`loadout/README.md`, rule 8).
