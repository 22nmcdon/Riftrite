# Legendary relics (14)

**The shop before each boss · 30 shards.** An engine that spans builds, or a big structural change. Rules for all relics: `README.md`. Numbers are placeholders.

## Chains

Each is a chain (`README.md`, rule 6), so Chain of Echoes (boss) makes it go deeper and grow.

| Relic | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Crown of Stars** | Crits can crit again, up to 10 times in a row. Each extra roll uses the hero's CRIT and crit bonus reduced by 5% per step in the chain (95%, 90%, 85%...). CRIT above 100% keeps the chain going longer |
| **Shared Pain** | Damage dealt to an enemy with a keyword echoes to every other enemy with that keyword, 10% weaker. Echoes can echo, up to 3 steps deep (90%, 81%, 73%). A hit echoes through one keyword only (the one the most other enemies share), and each enemy is hit at most once per step |
| **The Hungering Rift** | Overkill damage carries to the nearest enemy, and keeps carrying until it runs out |
| **Overcharge** | Mana past a full bar keeps filling; each extra full bar fires the signature again, +25% stronger each time |

## Keyword and lane engines

| Relic | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Warden's Engine** | Shields heroes give are 30% larger. Shielded heroes gain +% ATK and MGK equal to 5% of their Shield (a 400 Shield: +20%) |
| **Hunter's Engine** | Marks stack instead of refreshing. Each Mark stack on an enemy gives crits against it +20% damage. A crit on a Marked enemy Marks every enemy within 1 hex |
| **Shadow Engine** | While hidden, overhealing from lifesteal hits the hero's target for 500% of the overheal. Basic attacks while hidden deal +100% damage and have 5% lifesteal |
| **Rift-Fed Blades** *(growing)* | Heroes gain +1% ATK for every 1,000 basic-attack damage dealt this run. Only ATK, so MGK-based abilities don't benefit |
| **Quickening** | Every hit gives the hero +1% attack speed for the rest of the fight, with no cap |
| **Second Dawn** | The first time each hero falls in a fight, they rise 5s later at 50% HP. If every hero is down at the same moment, the fight is lost, even with a rise still waiting |

## Relic collection

| Relic | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Reliquary** | Every common relic you own is twice as strong |
| **Reliquary Lamp** | Heroes gain +2% to all basic stats for every relic you own |
