# Sigils (15)

**8 shards.** A sigil changes how the hero's signature fires. Written against "your signature", so it survives a transformation. Rank II after 10 casts of the signature, rank III after 25 more; buying a copy skips a rank. A sigil that doesn't apply to a hero's signature (mana sigils on an HP-triggered signature, say) simply does nothing. Rules: `README.md`. Numbers are placeholders.

## How it fires

| Sigil | Rank I | Rank II | Rank III |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Echo** | Fires again 2s later at 40% | At 60% | 1s later, at 60% |
| **Opener** | Starts every fight with 50% mana | 75% | 100% |
| **Desperate** | Also fires once when you first drop below 40% HP | Below 50% | Below 50%, and also gives you a Shield of 10% of your max HP |
| **Thrift** | Costs 15% less mana | 25% less | 35% less |
| **Quickcast** | Cast time −30% | −50% | Instant |

## What it does

| Sigil | Rank I | Rank II | Rank III |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Wide** | Its area is 1 hex larger, or it hits 1 more target | Both | +2 hexes, or +2 targets |
| **Lingering** | Its areas and statuses last 1s longer | 2s | 3s |
| **Siphon** | It has 15% lifesteal | 25% | 35% |
| **Execution** | +30% damage to enemies below 30% HP | +45% | A kill with it refunds 25% of its mana |
| **Surge** | After it fires, +20% attack speed for 3s | +30% | +30% for 5s |

## Keywords

| Sigil | Rank I | Rank II | Rank III |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Tolling** | Marks everything it hits for 3s | 4s | 5s |
| **Kindled** | Applies 3 Burn to everything it hits | 5 Burn | 8 Burn |
| **Bulwark** | Gives you a Shield of 10% of your max HP | 15% | 15%, and allies within 1 hex get it too |
| **Grasping** | Roots everything it hits for 0.5s | 1s | 1.5s |
| **Veiled** | Hides you for 1s after it fires | 1.5s | 2s |

## Combos they open

- **Echo** with The Second Sun (boss): four casts per signature.
- **Siphon** with Blood Communion and Overflow Chalice (epics): signature lifesteal becomes Shields.
- **Veiled** is the first reliable Stealth for any hero, which makes Shadow Engine and Veil of the Lost real options.
- **Tolling** and **Grasping** put a keyword on a whole group at once with an area signature.
