# The build map

Status: **agreed in discussion (2026-10-02), not built.** The team builds the game should support, what each one needs, and where the roster stands. **The current heroes are not changed by this file;** it's the checklist for new heroes and their paths. Hero designs: `rebuild-heroes.md`. Relics: `relics/`. Loadout: `loadout/`.

## 1. Why

A team should feel like a **root team**, a **burn team**, or a **shield team**, not three heroes who happen to own the same relics. Today most synergy lives in relics and charms, which any team can buy. Heroes need to play off each other: every hero should have other heroes they're better with.

## 2. How a build works

Every build needs three jobs, ideally filled by **different heroes**:

| Job | What it does | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Maker** | Creates the condition | Applies Roots, Shields, Burn, Marks |
| **Payoff** | Gets stronger from the condition | Hits harder on Rooted enemies; scales from Burn near them |
| **Glue** | Keeps the build alive | Protects the payoff, buys time, holds enemies in place |

**Rules for the roster:**

1. **Every build needs at least 2 makers and 2 payoffs, spread over at least 2 heroes,** so a team can form around it without one hero doing everything.
2. **A hero who is both maker and payoff for the same build** (Ilse for Burn) is fine, but the build still needs another payoff on a different hero, so the team has a reason to exist together.
3. **Every new hero's three paths each touch a different build**, as maker or payoff. A new hero then opens several team combinations, not one.
4. **Relics and loadout items support builds; they never replace a hero's role in one.** A build whose only payoff is a relic counts as missing that payoff.
5. **No build may depend on a single hero.** Count paths and apexes (not upgrades or relics): every build needs makers on at least 2 heroes and payoffs on at least 2 heroes.
6. **Builds every run wants need three or more heroes.** Economy and Mana are wanted in every run, whatever the build, so they need makers on at least three heroes. Then no single hero becomes the always-pick.

### The scavenger (shard hero), first sketch

- **The trade-off:** a scavenger is **weaker in a fight** than the other heroes. You give up power now for a richer run later: the classic greed pick.
- **Base kit:** a scrappy scavenger, melee or short range, modest stats. **Passive, Pickings:** when an enemy dies within 2 hexes of them, +1 shard (up to 5 per fight).

| Path | Idea | Builds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hoarder** | Stronger the more shards you're *holding* (e.g. +1% ATK and DEF per 5 shards) | Economy payoff, alongside Gilded Rift and Miser's Vault |
| **Fence** | Rewards *spending*: cheaper rerolls, and every shard spent in a shop gives the team a small permanent stat bonus for the run | Economy; boosts every build through the shop |
| **Bounty Hunter** | Marks a bounty on the toughest enemy; killing it pays shards | Mark maker, bridging into the Mark build |

- **Guardrails:** a cap on shards earned per fight (so endless can't become infinite money); spending power stays in the run, never in meta progression (`CLAUDE.md`, rule 5); Hoarder and Fence pull opposite ways (save versus spend), so they make two different runs.

## 5. Build payoff relics (proposed)

A kind of relic that's missing: not "enemies with X take more damage", but **"every time your team does X, your team gets stronger"**, which ramps through a fight. One per build, at the epic tier, with a cap per fight (lifted in endless). First drafts:

| Relic | Build | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Huntsman's Horn** | Root | Each time a hero Roots an enemy, heroes gain +3% ATK and +3% attack speed for the rest of the fight (up to 15 times) |
| **Tally Drum** | Mark | Each time a hero Marks an enemy, heroes gain +4 CRIT for the rest of the fight (up to 10 times) |
| **Pyre Banner** | Burn | Every 10 Burn your heroes apply gives heroes +3% ATK and MGK for the rest of the fight (up to 15 times) |
| **Shieldbearer's Oath** | Shield | Each time a hero gains a Shield, heroes gain +2 DEF for the rest of the fight (up to 20 times) |
| **War Horn** | Clump | Each time a hero taunts, heroes gain +5% attack speed for 4s (stacks) |

The other builds (Rangers, Sustain, Stealth, Mana, Economy) get theirs once their heroes exist.

## 6. How to use this file

- **Designing a new hero:** say which builds each path makes or pays off, and update the table in section 3.
- **Designing a relic or item:** say which build it supports. A build with no hero payoff doesn't get more relic payoffs until it has one.
- **Testing:** the combo fixtures (part 7, section 7) get one team per build, so each build is checked to work.
- **Every path has a type** (self-sufficient, engine, enabler, or long-game), listed in `build-tuning.md`, section 6. A new hero's three paths should not all be one type.
