# Act 2: the Glassmere (a draft)

Status: **a draft, marked up by the playtester (2026-10-04; Decisions below, BT and BR still open). Nothing is built.** Names and numbers are placeholders. It follows `rebuild-phase8-acts.md` (section 2a and Decisions 1–18): 7 days, the board rule of shallow water, new faces for about half the archetypes and Act 1's enemies back in specialized form for the rest, the Summoner and the Splitter as new archetypes, 2 elites, and a boss built for a team with at least one apex. Questions are in section 10.

## 1. The act at a glance

- **Its question:** can you handle the terrain?
- **The place:** the rift's drowned middle. Flooded ruins, glassy water, cold light.
- **The days:** as Act 1's: normal, normal, elite, normal, elite, normal, boss.
- **The difficulty** (Decision 3): early on, a team with its first transformations wins about half its fights; by the boss, a team without an apex is near 20%, and at least one apex per team lands before it (Decision 2).
- **Enemy growth** (Decision 4): from day 3, about half of a fight's enemies carry a specialization.

## 2. Shallow water (the board rule)

- **What water is:** each encounter lists its water hexes, the way it lists rocks. A unit on water moves at half speed. A Burned unit standing in water takes half of each Burn tick; the Burn lasts as long as anywhere else (Decision 5). Shots, areas, leaps, pulls, and knockbacks ignore water.
- **What it asks:** where you stand. A hero on water is slow to get anywhere and slow to get away. A ford is a choke point. A pool between you and the archers is a long walk. Enemies that live in water are tougher to reach and weaker against Burn.
- **What it does to the paths:**
  - **Ironbrand's knockbacks** can push enemies into water, where they're slow, or out of it (the Drowned Warden, section 3).
  - **Trapper's snares** on the water's edge catch what wades slowly into them.
  - **Hearthwall** holds a ford: one lane, slow to cross.
  - **Deadeye and Vigil Keeper** want a dry corner with a long line across the water.
  - **Volley** needs dry ground to keep moving.
  - **Burn builds** (relics, items, later Ilse) are weaker against enemies standing in water, and stronger once those enemies are pulled out.
  - Enemy Burn (the Ashling) is weaker on heroes standing in water, so water can be shelter too.
- **Water that changes:** three fights change the water as they go. The Tidecaller floods a circle, the Tide Choir's tide rises, and the Mournwater drains and floods the board (sections 3, 6, 7).
- **Readability:** the board draws water. A unit in water shows a ripple. A Burn tick halved by water says so in the log ("in water").

## 3. The new faces

Each tests one positioning question, with answers in the paths. Each has two specializations, seen from day 3.

### Mire Eel (Flanker)

*Threat line:* "Swims under the water and surfaces behind you."

- **What it does:** fast (speed 3), and a swimmer (no slow in water). While on water it's **Submerged**: it can't be targeted, like Stealth. It surfaces to bite the back-liner nearest the water.
- **It tests:** keeping the back line away from the water's edge.
- **Answers:** Hearthwall covering the ford; Trapper's snares on the bank; Deadeye from a dry corner; any hero on the bank to catch it as it surfaces.
- **Specializations:**
  - **Ambush Eel:** its first bite from under the water Stuns for 1s.
  - **Coiling Eel:** its bite drags the hero 1 hex toward the water.

### Reedline Slinger (Ranged)

*Threat line:* "Lobs stones from the reeds at whoever stands together."

- **What it does:** range 4. It stands in water at the back and lobs a stone that lands as a warned 1-hex circle. When approached, it steps back through the water.
- **It tests:** closing distance through water, or spreading out so a stone hits one hero, not three.
- **Answers:** Brand Slam's leap over the water; Deadeye outranging it; Volley keeping on the move; spreading out.
- **Specializations:**
  - **Skipping Slinger:** a stone skips to a second hex a hex beyond.
  - **Mudslinger:** where a stone lands, the ground is mud for 3s, slowing by 30%.

### Tidecaller (Caster)

*Threat line:* "Floods the ground under your largest group."

- **What it does:** signature on mana. It floods a 2-hex circle on your largest group for 6s: water while it lasts. Its basic attack deals 30% more to units standing in water.
- **It tests:** not bunching, and not standing still in its flood.
- **Answers:** spreading out; killing it fast (Deadeye, Casters first); Volley's movement; Lanternbearer keeping the slowed group standing.
- **Specializations:**
  - **Undertow Tidecaller:** its flood pulls everyone in it 1 hex toward the middle as it lands.
  - **Stilling Tidecaller:** heroes in its flood gain no mana while they stand in it.

### Drowned Warden (Anchor)

*Threat line:* "Holds the pool, and the pool holds it up."

- **What it does:** taunts heroes within 2 hexes, like the Sentinel. While it stands in water it regains 2% of its max HP a second and takes 20% less damage.
- **It tests:** fighting it out of the water, or burning it down fast.
- **Answers:** Ironbrand's knockbacks pushing it onto dry ground; Trapper rooting it after it steps out; Marked focus fire; Inquisitor's execute.
- **Specializations:**
  - **Tidebound Warden:** in water, its taunt reaches 3 hexes.
  - **Silted Warden:** where it walks, it leaves water for 5s, a pool that follows it.

## 5. The new archetypes

### Drowned Bellringer (Summoner)

*Threat line:* "Rings up the drowned from the water."

- **What it does:** stands at the back. Every 8s it rings, and a **Drowned Thrall** (a weak swimmer: melee, about a fifth of a Pup's HP and twice its attack) climbs out of the water hex nearest a hero. At most 4 thralls stand at once.
- **It tests:** target priority (the bell, not the thralls), and placing heroes away from water.
- **Answers:** Deadeye's Marked shot; Casters first (if the tactic counts Summoners as casters: question BU); Brand Slam leaping to it; Vigil Keeper's Sunfall line through the thralls to it.
- **Specializations:**
  - **Tolling Bellringer:** each ring also heals its thralls by 20%.
  - **Deep Bell:** rings every 12s, but two thralls rise.

### Glass Shambler (Splitter)

*Threat line:* "Breaks into two when it breaks."

- **What it does:** slow and sturdy. When it falls it splits into two **Glass Shards**: full enemies with 40% of its max HP and its attack. Shards don't split again (Decision 16: the halves are full enemies).
- **It tests:** burst against area damage. Single-target damage makes more enemies; area damage clears the halves together.
- **Answers:** Volley and Hailstorm; Ironbrand's cleave and Forgebreaker; Sunfall's line; Lanternbearer outlasting a crowd.
- **Specializations:**
  - **Jagged Glass:** its shards deal 30% more.
  - **Clouded Glass:** its shards are born Stealthed for 2s.

## 6. The elites

Each is built around one mechanic, says what it does, and is previewed days ahead.

### The Tide Choir, "Rising Tide"

- **Who:** a Tidecaller (the leader), two Drowned Bellringers, and a Drowned Warden.
- **The mechanic:** every 15s the water rises: every pool spreads one hex outward. Dry ground shrinks from the inside while Rift Collapse eats the edge, and thralls have more water to rise from.
- **What answers it:** winning before the tide is high, so killing the bells first; holding the dry middle (the encounter puts rocks there to anchor it); Hearthwall's walls and Guard keeping the dry ground yours.

### The Glass Matron, "Unbroken Glass"

- **Who:** a great Glass Shambler (the leader) and two Glass Shamblers, by a wide pool.
- **The mechanic:** a Glass Shard that falls on water reforms into a Glass Shambler after 4s. One that falls on dry ground stays broken.
- **What answers it:** holding the fight on dry ground. Hearthwall's Guard at the bank, Ironbrand knocking them out of the water, Trapper rooting them on the dry side, and area damage to break the shards together where they fall.

With only two elites, both are offered on each elite day (question BO).

## 7. The boss: the Mournwater

The drowned heart of the Glassmere: an old colossus lying in the deep water at the back. It's built to be fought by a team with at least one apex (Decision 2).

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Start** | Half the board is water. The Mournwater lies in the deep pool at the back with two Drowned Bellringers at the edges. **Undertow** (every 8s): every hero standing on water is pulled 1 hex toward her. |
| **Below 60%: the Mere Drains** | The water drains back to a ring around her. She rises and walks out, and every 10s a Glass Shambler crawls from the drained ground. |
| **Below 25%: the Flood** | Everything but the rocks floods. Everyone is at half speed, Burn is halved, Undertow pulls every hero every 6s, and Rift Collapse starts at once. |

- **What she tests:** placing off the water; then target priority on a crowding board; then a damage race on a flooded board, where an apex is what wins it.
- **What answers it:**
  - **Start:** dry corners for the back line (Deadeye, Eagle Eye); Hearthwall's walls between the pool and the team; Wardweaver's Shields against the pulls.
  - **The drain:** area damage for the Shamblers.
  - **The flood:** sustain and burst (The Beacon, Undying Oath, Forgebreaker, Hailstorm).

## 9. What it needs in the sim

Each piece is skipped by a fight that doesn't use it, so Act 1's fights and the bench's fingerprints stay as they are.

1. **Water hexes** (`"water"` on an encounter, like rocks): half speed (and the route's cost), Burn ticks halved on a unit standing in water (STATUS_DAMAGE noted "in water"), and the board draws it.
2. **`on_water`**, a condition (`UnitCondition`, an aura's `"while"`): the Warden's regeneration, the Eel's Submerge, Undertow's targets, and the Steaming Ashling.
3. **Swimmers**, a trait: no slow in water (the Eel, thralls, the Mournwater).
4. **Water that changes in a fight:** a new effect, `flood` (a circle of water for a time, every pool one hex wider, the whole board, or drained to a ring), with a new log kind (WATER: an audit rule and a board form). No existing effect changes the ground, so this one is new code.
5. **A summon at the water hex nearest a hero** (the Bellringer, the boss).
6. **Rising again if it fell on water** (the Matron's shards): the rise passive with a condition and a kit to rise as.
7. **Splitting** is `on_fall` with a summon of two shards, built pieces, unless the build finds otherwise.

## Decisions

The playtester, 2026-10-04:

1. **Heroes can be placed on water** (Question BM); they start slow.
2. **Crumbled ground over water both hurts and slows** (Question BN).
3. **Two elites are enough for now** (Question BO): each elite day offers both.
4. **Upgrades start in Act 3, for now** (Question BP): Act 2's elites and boss carry none (`rebuild-phase8-acts.md`, Decision 4, already names Act 3).
5. **The names stand for now** (Question BQ).
6. **Specializations are drawn fresh** (Question BS): each time a fight is offered, not fixed per encounter, so the player can't know them in advance ("you shouldn't know what it is beforehand"). On normal difficulty they're revealed on the fight's card on the day it's offered (the route), so choosing between the day's two fights and placing can answer them; the fight card still shows every specialization (`enemy-growth.md`, section 1). Hidden until the fight starts is a candidate difficulty modifier (`rebuild-enemies.md`, section 8).
7. **Casters first counts Summoners** (Question BU): the Drowned Bellringer is one of the tactic's targets.
